# WAP-7 running-gear reconstruction: evidence and limits

This is original editable geometry. No source photograph is applied to the mesh. Photographs and third-party training slides in the working research folder are inspection references, not redistributable textures. Do not bundle the research images with the release unless their licences are separately cleared.

## Authoritative geometry / mechanical evidence

1. **RDSO, WAP-7 upgrade specification, 2017, section 3.2**
   https://rdso.indianrailways.gov.in/works/uploads/File/17042017_Final_draft_Specification_%20Upgradation_WAP7%281%29.pdf
   - Existing bogie is a fabricated steel box structure, not a cast sideframe
   - Three axles, helical primary and secondary suspension, axlebox longitudinal guide links with spheribloc bushes, axle-hung nose-suspended motors
   - Section 3.2.10: solid rolled steel wheel, nominal new diameter 1,092 mm; gauge-face/back-to-back distance 1,596 mm with stated tolerance; wear-adapted profile SK.DL-2561
   - The exact SK.DL-2561 coordinate table was not available. This model preserves the nominal tread diameter and gauge-face distance and supplies a plausible smooth flange/root/tread and dished forged web. It is not a machining template or engineering-certified wheel contour

2. **RDSO, approved WAP-7 upgrade specification, May 2025, section 3.2.19 (printed p43)**
   https://rdso.indianrailways.gov.in/uploads/files/20250501_128_Rev1_Approved%20Specification_WAP7_To%20be%20uploaded%20on%20RDSO%20website.pdf
   - Existing bogie dimensions: headstock frame length 6,209 mm; bare frame width 2,480 mm; wheelbase 3,700 mm; primary and secondary spring transverse centre spacing 2,240 mm
   - Module uses those fixed class-level dimensions. The headstock extent is not confused with the 5,930 mm figure in the older brake conversion paper

3. **IRICEN, WAP7/WAG9 Three Phase Locomotive monograph, December 2023**
   https://iricen.gov.in/iricen/books_jquery/monograph_wap7_wap9.pdf
   - Chapter 10.1 and figure 40: end-axle single primary coils, middle-axle inner/outer nested coils, end-axle vertical dampers, middle-axle lifting safety link
   - Table 19: loaded primary outer spring height 192 mm; middle inner coil 188 mm
   - Chapter 10.2, figures 41–42 and tables 21–22: paired secondary springs, vertical/lateral/yaw dampers and chain; 251 mm mean spring diameter, 53 mm bar diameter, 9.5 total coils, 575 mm loaded height
   - The new secondary springs have the actual long loaded-height form and enter the body's engineered spring pockets. They are not shortened to the visible gap below the body
   - Official binary download was intermittently unavailable (502); indexed text was examined and cross-checked against primary-authored training figures noted below

4. **RDSO, Brake Rigging of WAP7 Locomotive, R. N. Lal and M. K. Gupta, pp6–7**
   https://rdso.indianrailways.gov.in/works/uploads/File/Brake%20Rigging%20of%20WAP7%20Locomotive(1).pdf
   - Distinguishes original hanging TBU/PBU equipment from conventional converted WAP-7 rigging
   - Conventional system has four top-mounted brake cylinders per bogie, connected levers, pull rods and curved tread brake shoes
   - Model deliberately uses only this conventional system. It does not simultaneously add TBU/PBU units or disc brakes
   - Small lever lengths, bends and pipe routing are representative because the exact numbered-locomotive drawing was unavailable

5. **CLW, Category Book II for 2019–2020, helical springs for WAP-7**
   https://clw.indianrailways.gov.in/works/uploads/File/Category%20Book-II%20for%202019-2020.pdf
   - Locomotive quantities confirm 16 end-axle primary springs, 8 middle-axle outer springs, 8 middle-axle inner springs and 8 secondary springs
   - Module follows these quantities. 'Inner' and 'outer' secondary wording is not used as authority to add fictitious nested secondary coils

6. **Central Railway / ZRTI Bhusawal, three-phase locomotive training manual**
   https://cr.indianrailways.gov.in/cris/uploads/files/1383198951757-ABB%20Loco%20in%20English.pdf
   - Printed p7 and p34: two main compressors are mounted under the machine-room bays
   - Printed p25: two battery boxes, one on each side
   - Printed p59: WAP7/WAG9 main air reservoirs are upright inside the machine room. The underfloor-tank arrangement belongs to WAP5 and is deliberately absent from this module
   - This class distinction was checked explicitly rather than carrying over the old model's underfloor reservoirs

7. **ELGi, Railway Compressors, official manufacturer brochure, printed p2; RR20100 CG-M product page**
   https://www.elgi.com/me/wp-content/uploads/2019/04/Railway-Compressors.pdf
   https://www.elgi.com/in/rr-20100-cg-m/
   - WAP7 is an identified application for the underslung RR20100 CG-M family
   - W-type, three cylinders, direct coupling, finned motor/cylinders and external cooler/filter assemblies inform the geometry
   - The CG-M reference envelope is 1450 × 871 × 768 mm, distinct from the smaller OF-M variant. Vehicle hanging brackets and exact installation offsets are representative
   - This is a compatible class-level compressor reconstruction; the actual maker/version fitted to 39002 was not established from its photograph

8. **CLW, WAG9 driver's manual, Figure 2.4 / chapter 2 p9, shared-platform underframe layout**
   https://www.scribd.com/document/430528811/Driver-Manual-for-Wag-9-Loco
   - Primary-authored manual available through a third-party public mirror
   - Corroborates the central aluminium transformer tank, two compressors in staggered side/end bays, two battery boxes in the other bays and an adjacent battery circuit-breaker enclosure
   - Component roles and arrangement are corroborated with the WAP7 training manual and actual WAP7 compressor photograph. Central tank/cabinet dimensions are representative because the factory dimensional GA was unavailable

## Model-specific interpretation

- The isolated running-gear module preserves its input root, body, bogie-yaw and axle-roll control transformations exactly. The integrated revision separately restores BODY scale Y to 1.0 and refits the cab frames; see the independent final controls audit
- Bogie centres remain at X = -6 and +6 m; axle offsets are -1.850, 0, +1.850 m
- New visual wheel diameter is 1.092 m at the nominal rail contact circle; inboard flange is larger, as it must be
- Axle boxes are non-rotating wing housings, while the one-piece wheels, hubs and shafts inherit axle roll
- Main body underframe and spring pockets are built by the exterior/coupler components; the module reports spring/cylinder clearance envelopes
- Fine fittings (sensor glands, exact bolt counts on representative housings, weld seams, pipe clamps, sand hopper latches, transformer exterior and hidden motor wiring) are plausible class-level detail rather than an authenticated 39002 maintenance reconstruction
- Paint, grime and wear are location-specific and restrained. No universal concrete-like noise layer is used

## Verification of the delivered module

The geometry revision with SHA256 `7d2a95226d4f0ee56e830b4df8b4650d83d9d6f9385d8870f7d096e1a4fccdbd` was checked in Blender 4.3.2:

- 4,394 new editable mesh objects, 461,120 raw vertices, 405,936 raw polygons / 906,424 raw triangles
- Zero non-manifold edges, zero zero-area faces and no negatively oriented closed mesh volumes in the generated geometry
- Applying the module a second time produces the same object count rather than duplicates
- Both bare fabricated bogie structures measure 6.209 m over headstocks and 2.480 m wide
- Complete running-gear plus central underfloor accessory width is 3.058 m; the flange extends below the nominal rail contact plane, as expected
- In the isolated running-gear test, input root/body/bogie/axle control matrices are unchanged; the integrated BODY/cab-frame corrections are recorded separately
- Axle-roll smoke test moved the wheel point while leaving the fixed frame unchanged; bogie-yaw test moved the frame while leaving body-mounted underfloor equipment unchanged
- All visual evidence was rendered directly from the editable geometry with Cycles CPU. No generated or photographic vehicle image was substituted

The public close-up gallery is rendered from the exact delivered master with `scripts/render_detail_gallery.py`. Available views include wheel, compressor, underfloor, bogie and roof electrical equipment. The integrated running gear uses the `surface_running_gear` dielectric paint/oxide roles and `surface_materials` tread/rubber/grease roles. The component's default materials remain a standalone fallback.

These are visual-source/model integrity checks, not mechanical stress, full swept-volume, production-manufacturing or game-runtime certification. The secondary suspension is a detailed visual reconstruction attached to the existing simplified rig; it is not a deforming spring simulation.
