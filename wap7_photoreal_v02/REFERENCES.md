# References and provenance

The component notes identify the photographs, railway documents and equipment-family sources used for the model, with the limits of each source:

- [Exterior, roof, coupler and dimensions](docs/EXTERIOR_REFERENCE_NOTES.md)
- [Driving cabs and instruments](docs/CAB_REFERENCE_NOTES.md)
- [Running gear and underfloor equipment](docs/RUNNING_GEAR_REFERENCE_NOTES.md)
- [Machinery-room arrangement](docs/MACHINERY_REFERENCE_NOTES.md)
- [Vehicle surface materials and wear](docs/SURFACE_MATERIALS.md)

The main identity/livery reference is [Royapuram WAP-7 39002 photographed by WAM4ajj](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:RPM_WAP-7.jpg), dated 21 November 2020. Its visible fittings and service condition inform a representative model. Research photographs and manual pages are linked as evidence; their image pixels are not redistributed in this package or used as vehicle textures.

Vehicle shader networks, instrument faces, decals, crest approximation and wear maps are original project artwork. The small Royapuram crest is an interpretation rather than an official logo asset. Reproducible artwork records are included for [cab instruments](textures/cab/PROVENANCE.json), [buffer contact maps](textures/surfaces/SURFV02_MAP_PROVENANCE.json) and [body/roof/windscreen service wear](textures/surfaces/SURFV02_SERVICE_WEAR_PROVENANCE.json).

The outdoor setting uses [Poly Haven's Kloofendal 48d Partly Cloudy Pure Sky](https://polyhaven.com/a/kloofendal_48d_partly_cloudy_puresky) by Greg Zaal and Jarod Guest, and [Dirt](https://polyhaven.com/a/dirt) by Charlotte Baglioni. Both are provided under [CC0](https://polyhaven.com/license). See [environment credits](environment/README.md) and the [verified dependency manifest](environment/dependency_manifest.json).

Sky and ground textures provide lighting and surface appearance on actual rendered geometry. Track, sleepers, ballast, vegetation, overhead equipment and generic depot buildings are modelled scene elements. The depot is not an as-built depiction of Royapuram. The machinery cutaway uses explicitly recorded presentation-only visibility changes.

Detailed or numerically consistent geometry is not proof of unit-exact authenticity. Hidden hardware, equipment-vendor details, attachment methods and the wheel contour remain representative where exact drawings or measured observations were unavailable.
