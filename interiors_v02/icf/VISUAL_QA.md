# Interior v02 visual review

The completed full-shell aisle and bay images were inspected at image-pixel level.

- Aisle is continuous with no bulkhead closing off the saloon
- Folded middle cushions form backrests; lower and upper cushions are separate
- Main-bay ladders remain to the main-berth side of the longitudinal aisle
- Barred windows, sill and inner shutter guides remain visible from inside
- Interior wall surfaces and ceiling read correctly from the passenger camera
- Main-bay lower seat footwell was widened during review without moving the exterior window grid
- Entry inner door panels were split around the window aperture rather than covering it
- Upper berth tie supports were extended to the curved ceiling instead of ending in free space
- Final views retain visible sampling noise; no denoising or image retouching was used

Full-shell passenger images do not hide any model geometry. The overview is explicitly a cutaway, with roof/ceiling and the near outer wall removed temporarily for inspection; the complete saved model restores them.

Exterior regression and cutaway overview were also inspected. The exterior remains enclosed with the original livery, bogies, wheels and proportions. The cutaway shows nine repeated sleeper bays, overhead fan units, an uninterrupted side aisle and simplified vestibule/toilet zones. No obvious interior protrusion through the exterior or berth-to-berth clipping was observed. The cutaway is diagnostic, not an alternate exported model.

Reopened saved Blend verification confirms zero render-hidden asset meshes, a relative render output path, 64 samples, two CPU threads and denoising disabled.
