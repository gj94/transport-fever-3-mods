# AMPA platform-edge clarification

The phrase “Enclosed hall shifted outside platform by geometric clearance” in the retained source-plan adjustment record describes the nominal enclosure adjustment. It does not mean the two complete polygons are disjoint.

Independent review measured approximately 7.47 m² of residual hall-envelope overlap along the western edge of platform body 2, locally about 0.40 m deep over local Y −47.6 to −21.0 m, plus a small end wedge. The intended scene treatment is a hall/plinth abutting that platform edge. Source platform and roof-envelope polygons remain retained; this is an estimated reconstruction, not a surveyed wall boundary.

The independent gate checks the remaining public walking width, rail clearance and scene circulation. This clarification supersedes an interpretation of the earlier adjustment wording as an exact zero-overlap claim. It changes no geometry, source-photo claim or rendered image.
