Immutable recovery checkpoint. The editable scene is built; final gallery, export and independent quality review are pending. This is not a final release. No source reference photographs are distributed.

Open `KAVR_coastal_station_v01.blend` in Blender. Exact source SHA256: `4976f53590e74b67003c6c95cffda6c4e1db9e57880e4aae804b300d279e4830`.
