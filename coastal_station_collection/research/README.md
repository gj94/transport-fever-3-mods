# Evidence register

Research compiled 8 October 2026 for the Ernakulam–Alappuzha–Thiruvananthapuram–Nagercoil coastal route. Primary map chainages, mixed-date OpenStreetMap observations, reported India Rail Info platform totals, Wikipedia revisions and visual observations are separate evidence layers.

The station register contains 55 active route station/halts plus the closed Tirunettur halt. Empty numeric fields mean unresolved, not zero. Reported platform totals do not establish exact platform geometry. Profile update dates are not necessarily the update date of any specific count. Proposed, commissioned, historical and separate-depot facilities are not silently summed.

The source ledger records provenance and dates. OpenStreetMap-derived data is attributed to © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database License: https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright and https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/. Wikipedia-derived observations retain source and revision links; underlying article content is available under CC BY-SA. Links and numerical observations from other sources are provided for verification; no third-party image redistribution rights are asserted. Raw Google imagery and third-party research photos are excluded from this collection.

Station models are interpretive reconstructions. Interiors, unsurveyed dimensions and uncertain yard details must be labelled by each station's README; this evidence package is not an engineering as-built certificate.
