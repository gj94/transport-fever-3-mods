# Ernakulam–Nagercoil coastal station collection

Actual-model images first; click an image for full size. This collection covers the 56 entries in the route register: 55 active station/halts and the separately labelled closed historical Tirunettur entry. ERS, TVC and NCJ retain their existing rich-v02 models; 52 other active entries and TNU are being developed here.

These are source models and portable exchange assets for further mod development. Native Transport Fever 3 runtime compatibility has not been tested. Mixed-date mapping, station photographs and reported platform figures guide the reconstructions; inferred interiors, unknown track details and historical status remain explicitly labelled. Do not treat them as surveyed or operationally certified railway plans.
