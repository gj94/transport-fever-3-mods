# Reference provenance and precision

Target: the white/blue **Vande Bharat 2.0 chair-car family**, using prototype 24m car pitch. This is not the later sleeper train. All supplied train geometry, display artwork and textile patterns are original procedural constructions. The source photographs and railway drawings were inspected, not redistributed as textures.

## Primary layout and dimensional source

[CAMTECH, September2022, VBE Trainset V2 System Documentation, official IRIMEE host](https://rskr.irimee.in/forum/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/Vande%20Bharat%20Trainset_Maintenance_Manual_Volume_II_System_Documentation.pdf)

One-based PDF pages:
-28: DTC layout TS/DTC-9-0-001, code889, including the16-car formation
-29: MC layout TS/MC-9-0-001, code890
-30: TC layout TS/TC-9-0-001, code891
-31: MC2 layout, code892
-32–33: NDTC/EC and NDTC/EC2 layouts, codes893/894
-410: Medha motor-bogie drawing D-A675UV2-132983 Rev01; related variants on411–412 and415–416

Explicit callouts used:24,000mm over couplers;23,100mm intermediate body;14,900mm bogie centers;2,700mm wheelbase;3,240mm body width;1,676mm gauge;952mm new wheels;1,320mm floor above rail. The motor bogie drawing shows four double-concentric primary-spring stations, two secondary bellows and750mm wheel-mounted brake discs.

Native embedded diagram labels were checked for glazing: ordinary1500×880mm; emergency assembly1580×900mm; saloon sill765mm above floor. Thus the ordinary pane spans railZ2.085–2.965m; the emergency assembly sharing that lower sill spans2.085–2.985m. The much larger2300/2330mm vertical cabin dimension is not window height.

The DTC note specifies **one lavatory**, opposite wheelchair maneuvering space. Intermediate cars have two toilets at one end and pantry/electrical rooms at the other; EC2 reverses its zoning. Physical seating is44 DTC,78 CC and52 EC.

[Independent CAMTECH Trainset Depot Facilities report, §7.3 / PDFp18](https://indianrailways.gov.in/railwayboard/uploads/directorate/eff_res/camtech/mechanical/YearWise/Report_Facilities_TrainsetDepot_V3.pdf) corroborates24m pitch,14.9m bogie centers and2.7m wheelbase across the car family.

### Exact dimensions versus inferred coordinates

The intermediate longitudinal chain is explicit:

`3.950 +0.165 +15.570 +0.165 +3.250 =23.100m`

With pantry+X/toilets−X, clear saloon limits are−8.135/+7.435m, and partition centers−8.2175/+7.5175m. EC2 reverses this arrangement.

The DTC23.328m nose-to-rear body callout and the14.9m bogie dimension calibrate the drawing. Model placements near nose+11.78, cab partition+7.30, cab door+8.25, passenger doors+4.03/−10.75 and saloon partitions+3.33/−7.86m are **drawing-coordinate estimates**, generally±50–100mm, not explicit manufacturing datums. Driver/desk positions are ergonomic interpretations within that shell.

Roof equipment centers are less precise, approximately±100–200mm: ordinary cars' HVAC near±10m; TC pantograph at the power end around+9.4m, switchgear+7.2..7.8m, HVAC+5.8/−10m; DTC main HVAC+5.85/−10.2m plus smaller cab AC+8.2m. Pantograph hinge hand is not unambiguously resolved by the schematic side view.

## Formation scope

The16-car order is shown in the primary system layout. Its total is1,128=2×44+12×78+2×52. The [South Central Railway operating certificate](https://digitalscr.in/bzadiv/circulars/misc_circulars/uploads/Vandebharat_GMsanction.pdf) also supports the formation.

The [North Western Railway5July2023 circular, hosted scan](https://st.indiarailinfo.com/kjfdsuiemjvcya24/0/6/9/2/5769692/0/commhq1764176322411285603.pdf) supports the8-car inventory. The detailed numbered order/handing of the supplied8-car derivative remains a selected interpretation. Its530-seat total is2×44+5×78+52; TC_EC combines that supported role with the52-seat EC layout.

## Air suspension

[RDSO January2024 specification, §2.3](https://rdso.indianrailways.gov.in/uploads/Reasoned%20Document%20on%20Final%20STR%20RDSO2020CG-01%2016_01_2024__EMU_DEMU%20US.pdf): two secondary air springs per bogie/four per coach. Detailed suspension hardware beyond the visible drawing forms is representative, not a certified exact subtype.

## Pantograph and roof electrics

[Schunk WBL22.03 OEM manual, drawingSB-035320, official IRIMEE host](https://rskr.irimee.in/forum/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/Operational%20Manual%20Pantograph%20_Train%2018.pdf): p16 gives1.800m overall panhead width,35mm carbon strips and390mm center spacing. Assembly views are onp19, base/lower/upper frame on20–22, flexible copper bearing shunts on34, and head on29 and125. Flat leaf-spring packs, rocker cheeks, carrier clamps, pneumatic monitoring lines, eye-lug shunts and bolted insulator feet follow those views. Flexible connections are Hook-deformed to the authored linkage; they are visual approximations, not an engineering simulation.

[Central Railway VB2 parts procurement, item208](https://cr.indianrailways.gov.in/uploads/files/1754293157975-Apr25ns.pdf) identifies WBL22.03 and SK1590/SK85CU strips. This supports using the earlier OEM component manual for the modelled VB2 family.

The TC system drawing labels4.260m folded pantograph height. OEM585mm resting height is relative to its mounting reference and must not be treated as a rail height. The model's1.5/1.2m rigid linkage and5.917m raised target remain authored approximations; its component detail and folded envelope do not establish complete OEM kinematics.

[CAMTECH V2 propulsion chapter](https://rdso.indianrailways.gov.in/uploads/Trainset%28V2%29_Maintenance_Manual_Volume_II_Chapter_5_Maintenance_Propulsion_System.pdf) identifies the Schneider22CB roof circuit-breaker family.

Full-rake presentation adapts the repository's WAP7v02 original railway/depot geometry. Its separately supplied [Poly Haven Kloofendal pure sky](https://polyhaven.com/a/kloofendal_48d_partly_cloudy_puresky) and soil maps retain their existing CC0 attribution and files in `../wap7_photoreal_v02/environment/`. ### Schneider 22CB installation detail

[Schneider Electric 22CB maintenance manual, June 2022, official IRIMEE host](https://rskr.irimee.in/forum/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/LOCO-New%20Manual%20-%20DTD%2010.08.22.pdf), printed pages7 and10 (PDF11 for the upright outline), distinguishes the upper incoming terminal, middle outgoing terminal and separate earthing contacts. The VB-specific photograph in the system manual, PDF315 / electrical chapter page35, corroborates the stacked ribbed porcelain sections and orange terminal castings. The model includes six baseplate fixings, under-roof mechanism housing, terminal hardware, open earth arms, air fitting and connector. Its approximately507mm above-plate tower and178mm below-plate box are drawing-informed proportions; roof saddles and precise pipe routing remain representative.

The curved roof end caps close the shell above the end-wall lintel while leaving the central gangway passage open, following the continuous capped end profile in the TC drawing.
