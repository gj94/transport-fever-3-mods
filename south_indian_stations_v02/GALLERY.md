# Detailed station gallery

Actual-model renders and explicitly labelled schematic plans. Click an image to inspect it at full size. No rolling stock. [Scope and opening instructions](README.md).

Mixed-date visual reconstruction; inferred interiors and engineering details are documented. Accepted earlier views of unchanged areas keep their original per-frame source lineage. Cycles grain remains in some interiors; TVC local Eevee views identify their engine in adjacent JSON. No claim of engineering or exact historical as-built fidelity.
