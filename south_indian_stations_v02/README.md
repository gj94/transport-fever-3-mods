# TVC, NCJ and ERS · detailed full-station reconstructions

## [Browse the rendered interiors, platforms and yards](GALLERY.md)

Three editable metre-scale Blender scenes expand the earlier frontage studies into furnished interiors, full mapped station approaches, platforms, sidings, reconstructed pointwork and station facilities. **No trains or rolling stock.** Earlier station studies and all vehicle assets remain unchanged.

- [TVC: heritage frontage, five platform faces and extensive mapped yard](tvc/README.md) · [detailed view guide](tvc/GALLERY.md)
- [NCJ: historical facade, side/island platforms, terminal bay and depot sidings](ncj/README.md)
- [ERS: west/east public interiors, six platform faces and staggered mapped yard](ers/README.md)

## Open the models and portable exports

Large files in this repository use exact-byte `.parts` folders. **They are not individually openable models.** [Reassembly instructions](REASSEMBLY.md) reconstruct and SHA256-verify each whole `.blend` or compressed export. Whole editable `.blend` files are saved in Library, but the chat attachment size limit prevented direct attachment. The repository parts reconstruct the same complete model files without loss. Every station includes procedural sources, source notices and validation records.

Portable glTF exports are supplied for interchange. These scenes are **not native Transport Fever 3 station mods** and have not been tested in that game's runtime. Visual detail and large editable scenes are not a claim of game-ready optimization, signalling simulation or certified clearances.

## Accuracy and review limits

TVC uses 2022 architectural references, NCJ uses older historical frontage references, and ERS uses 2017 architectural references. Yard coordinates come from a later OpenStreetMap snapshot. These are mixed-date visual reconstructions, not exact historical as-built surveys. Interior plans, many fixtures, operational labels, pit assignments and some compound pointwork are inferred and documented.

Gallery captions and per-frame provenance distinguish latest scenes from accepted views of unchanged rooms/rail geometry made before later localized fixes. Some Cycles interiors retain visible grain; some interior views use Eevee with their engine recorded. Annotated plans are schematics, not beauty renders, and review identifiers are not railway operational numbers.

[Final transfer and package validation](FINAL_VALIDATION.md), [independent evidence review](research/FINAL_AUDIT.md) and [station-source evidence](research/layout_evidence_audit.md) describe scoped checks and uncertainties. The independent audit records its review-time state; subsequent finishing/export checks are in each station's final QA and source-lineage files. No blanket engineering or collision certification is implied.

No new licence is granted for generated assets. Preserve OpenStreetMap attribution and ODbL provenance, font notices and the individual source records. Raw third-party reference photographs and report PDFs are excluded.
