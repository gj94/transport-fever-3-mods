# ERS v02 quality checks

## Geometry and evidence checks
- Architectural identity inspected against the 2017 west-front photograph and 2017 platform/footbridge photographs: blue parabolic portal and diagonal legs, clock, trilingual identity, differentiated small/broad arched gallery bays, grey/blue corrugated roofs and lattice footbridge language retained.
- Current OSM plan inspected as an actual plotted image; four distinct/staggered platform polygons preserved, serving six faces. Official Ministry of Railways evidence distinguishes 10 station lines/six platform lines from the separate coaching depot.
- All map vertices participate in graph adjacency. The earlier endpoint-only interpretation was rejected because it could mislabel connected track as a dead end. Buffers now appear only at retained true graph leaves on siding/yard routes.
- Plain-track gauge measured from the actual global mesh footprint at 26 unambiguous cross-sections: target 1.676 m, maximum measured deviation 0.00000065 m in the six-decimal source mesh. Four multi-route cross-sections are explicitly excluded from plain-track gauge measurement rather than reporting a misleading minimum across unrelated heads.
- All 43 route flange channels checked against actual generated head/blade mesh triangles: no substantive interior overlap after 2 µm quantization tolerance. Head/blade pieces partition the same union without duplicate overlays. Mesh top normals corrected to+Z; polygon exteriors and holes consistently oriented for sidewalls.
- Original local crossing-gap and isolated-nose prototypes were rejected after close-up review. The final loader removes their geometry and uses global rail-head/web unions with all-route45mm channels. This matters at compound crossings where a local patch could otherwise fill a third route's flange passage.
- Duplicated individual-track sleeper/fastener boxes trimmed inside turnout envelopes; common extended bearers and common ballast generated. These are visual reconstructions, not manufacturer tie schedules.
- West-platform ramp and east-entry threshold overlap their platform geometry. North bridge moved to x100 to keep the PF6 stair within its short platform. Stair-side bottom chord and vertical lattice posts removed from landing openings. East-entry noticeboards moved outside the doorway.
- Workshop relocated to x505–535/y161–175 after testing all mapped rails, with a 5 m clear bounding envelope. East boundary fence moved to y160; workshop gate preserved.
- Every OHE support candidate tested against all retained track centrelines and stair envelopes; safe alternative sides tried and unsupported candidates omitted. Continuous core/suspension/registration links added. Contact wire 6.2 m, messenger 6.9–7.2m and bridge underside 7.58 m avoid the earlier wire/deck intersection.
- Water main classification uses a 2.5 m all-track proximity threshold with 2 m sample intervals, dips to buried z−.30 at rail approaches and omits unsafe risers. Independent segment checking found the resulting exposed segments/transitions at least 2.2399 m from rail centrelines. Ducts and drain walls are recessed below ballast/sleeper elevations. These are gross interference checks, not railway clearance certification.
- No rolling stock is modelled. Coach-position labels identify boarding positions and are not platform numbers or rolling-stock objects.

## Explicit limits
This is a detailed visual asset, not a railway design, interlocking simulation, dimensional survey or verified historical as-built. The2017 architectural baseline, mixed-date mapped plan and reconstructed fixture/interior/hardware placements are separately labelled in SOURCES.md and COVERAGE.md. No future redevelopment architecture is presented as completed.

## Full-flight circulation check and narrow scene lineage
After the accepted rail/formation proof, a denser actual-scene check found two low platform-number boards, their associated pipes, and one bench encroaching on lower stair circulation. Only those fixture components were moved; rails, platform bodies, buildings and canopy geometry were not changed. `scene_lineage.json` records every moved component and the exact parent/corrected scene hashes. Unaffected earlier views retain their original parent-scene hash; the affected platform and bridge views are refreshed from the corrected scene.

The final flight test casts 111,720 vertical rays over all eight full stair flights, sampling about 50 mm longitudinally and laterally across 2.4 m width. It includes actual canopy/bridge meshes and swept pipe curves. No overhead/fixture obstruction remains. The report separately preserves contacts exactly at the clear-width boundary with the stairs' own supporting side stringers, less than 60 mm above tread level; these are classified as floor-edge structure, not silently dropped or described as canopy clashes. This does not constitute an accessibility or structural-design certificate.
