## Accuracy boundaries
Current cartographic data is not an engineering survey or proof of historical as-built conditions. Nominal track details and reconstructed interiors are visual modelling assumptions. Passing a geometric or visual review does not certify railway operation, electrical clearance, structure, accessibility or safety. The files preserve mixed-date evidence and station-specific uncertainty rather than claiming exact engineering replication.
