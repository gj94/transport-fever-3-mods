# Full-station layout evidence audit
Research date: 2026-10-08. Scope: TVC, NCJ, ERS full station reconstruction, no trains.

## Critical interpretation
These are visual reconstructions using mixed-date evidence, not measured as-built surveys. OSM rail ways are portions of a network, NOT a count of station tracks. OSM last-edit dates are NOT construction dates. Building photo dates do NOT date the entire rail yard. No signed dimensioned historical track plan was authenticated in this pass. Do not label current-map rail geometry as a 2010/2017/2022 exact yard.

Recommended baselines: TVC November2022 architectural appearance; ERS November2017 architecture; NCJ historical low-rise frontage with contemporary map-derived yard and documented bay, explicitly mixed-date pre-final-remodelling study. If a single historic date is mandatory, unverified later approach modifications must be separately reconstructed or omitted with disclosure.

- Primary Southern Railway GM report2015–16 identifies coaching-depot watering between road12/13 and road14/15 (PDF index161,167). This supports depot-road identity, not mapping those numbers onto unlabelled OSM ways.
  https://sr.indianrailways.gov.in/cris/uploads/files/1688799706975-gm%20report%202015%2016.pdf
- State2024 Nemom SIA says TVC pitlines are non-standard and require replacement. A generic maintenance-pit module must not be described as exact engineering.
  https://cmd.kerala.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/Nemom-Terminal-English-Report-Final.pdf
- Circa2013 Knight Frank study (only unauthenticated Scribd mirror retrieved) differentiates heritage main entry/ticketing/security/waiting/refreshments, separate reservation centre/ATM/IRCTC/cloakroom and second entry/tickets/restrooms. Proposed mall/hotel/bridges are NOT existing assets.
  https://www.scribd.com/document/341375437/Commerical-Exploitation-Report
- OSM local XML audit: most yard ways updated2020; some2023; approaches/splits2024;2/3 and4/5 platform objects editedJune2026. Current retrieval is not a historical snapshot.

  https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/chennai/locomotive-rams-platform-at-nagercoil-junction/articleshow/47073283.cms

  https://www.scribd.com/document/891476533/5627856413597001465511002244
- Railwhispers2023 advocacy report says1A connected only to the Thiruvananthapuram line; no exact throat geometry verified.
  https://railwhispers.com/?amp=&noamp=mobile&p=7578
- Purported2026 Annexure: existing single+island platform bodies; proposed single+two islands. Existing9 passenger roads including1A vs proposed7; five pits,9 new stabling lines in proposed state. It is text, not a dimensioned diagram; not authenticated or proof of commissioning. Do NOT construct all proposed points as existing.
  https://www.scribd.com/document/1044869637/NCJ-Annexure-a-2026
- Primary2015–16 SR report identifies pit1 as road9 and watering between8/9. This does not date every2026 facility to2015.
- Independent map_trace.png pixel inspection: two long platform polygons, west-side entrance, large east yard, northern triangular approach, curving southern approach. OSM polygon length is geometry-derived, not official usable platform length. XML has platform edits2021/2023 but substantial approach ways editedOct/Nov2025. Consequently the raw map is unsuitable to claim a2010 or2015 yard.
-2023/2024 Commons platform metadata verified, but image downloads failed; no claim of independent pixel inspection of those photos.

## ERS
- Independent osm_review.png pixel inspection confirms side1, island2/3, island4/5, side6 with longitudinal staggering. Do not make six islands or normalize lengths.
- Primary2015–16 SR report names paired PF2/3 paving, PF4/5 shelter/raising, and FOB extension to eastern Karshaka Road. Construction/work-list entries do not alone certify final dimensions.
- Primary NTCA-hosted Sabarimala transport masterplan, printed73/PDF index75, describes existing east-entry ticket counter, passenger waiting hall, siding with passenger platform and large front parking. This is pre2017 evidence for modelling those functions, not a floor plan.
  https://ntca.gov.in/assets/uploads/Reports/sabarimala/Vol2_Traffic_transportation.pdf
-2025 report describes moving accident-relief stabling to VIP siding by PF1 before extending PF2 southward. Thus present-day PF2 southern throat cannot automatically be assigned to2017.
  https://www.manoramaonline.com/district-news/ernakulam/2025/08/31/ernakulam-junction-platform-extension.html
- CUSAT thesis contains separate Ernakulam passenger rake-yard facility diagram(two650m pits and service buildings). Do not transplant ERSD facilities into ERS station yard. PDF pixels could not be retrieved, so not used for exact placement.
  https://dyuthi.cusat.ac.in/jspui/bitstream/purl/2818/1/Dyuthi-T0838.pdf
- OSM platform edits2023–24; several railway ways editedNovember2025 andJuly2026. Architectural2017 baseline remains distinct from current map-derived tracks.

## Geometry and provenance safeguards
Use geometry-derived dimensions only as approximate cartographic values; mark inferred heights/room partitions/canopy bays/game clearances separately. Platform edging, track gauge, sleeper profile, switch blades/frogs and pits can be fully modelled without pretending unverified station-specific dimensions are known. Empty tracks only; no rolling stock.
Retain OpenStreetMap attribution and ODbL provenance for raw/derived map data. Preserve raw inputs separately from distributable geometry with a source notice; don't silently relicense OSM-derived data under project-only terms. Commons images have individual licences; no raw photographs or source-rendered pixels were downloaded into this audit folder. All Scribd/report/news sources are reference links, not texture licences.
