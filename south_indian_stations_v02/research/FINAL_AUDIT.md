# Final independent review
Completed 8 October 2026, 15:32 UTC.

## Result
The core TVC, NCJ and ERS scenes pass this visual-reconstruction review for station coverage, platform organization, representative physical pointwork and the corrected circulation/service issues identified during construction. This is not an engineering survey, safety certificate or exhaustive collision certification.

TVC still requires its queued final bake/export synchronization after stone-scatter and general prop-clearance tidies. Its accepted pointwork is unchanged by those staged changes. Publication/export synchronization for every station remains the builder/publisher's responsibility.

## Station coverage
- **TVC:** three platform bodies serving five faces: side 1, islands 2/3 and 4/5. Map-derived tracks and service-yard fans are retained. Furnished entrance, separate booking area, waiting, office, toilets and service spaces are reconstructed. Eight independent scene-ray checks passed the entrance, hall-to-platform and six bridge stair entries.
- **NCJ:** side 1, island 2/3 and a terminal 1A bay, rather than four parallel through platforms. Twenty simple reconstructed switch envelopes and fourteen explicitly approximate compound/short-junction treatments are documented. Furnished rooms, bridge access and maintenance facilities are included. Independent office/bridge rays passed; final worker validation reports six clear circulation rays.
- **ERS:** side 1, islands 2/3 and 4/5, and side 6. Unequal lengths and staggered mapped footprints are preserved. Western and eastern public interiors, sanitary/service spaces, bridge connections and a relocated rail-clear workshop are included.

No rolling stock was identified in the inspected source, scene inventories or review images.

## Physical rail and service checks
TVC and ERS actual serialized head/blade meshes were independently reconstructed as planar surfaces and tested against every generated flange channel. Both had zero channel-interior intrusion at a 2 micrometre rounding tolerance, with effectively zero head/blade overlap. The generic 45 mm channel target is grounded in the cited Indian railway 44–48 mm range; it is not evidence of each station's actual turnout design.

Actual accepted pointwork proofs:
- TVC: `15_Frog_closeup.png` and `10_Turnout_detail.png`
- NCJ: `17_PHYSICAL_TURNOUT_6045464001.png`, `18_FROG_DETAIL_6045464001.png`, `19_SWITCH_TOE_6045464001.png`
- ERS: corrected `13_Turnout_frog_detail.png`, with its frozen-scene proof record

The proofs show supporting running rails, crossing noses/wing rails, flange channels, check rails and bearer geometry. They replace earlier rejected overlay/broken-rail versions. NCJ's wide and close views also show the reconstructed tongue/stretcher arrangement.

Identified obstructions were corrected: false buffer classification, gaps at station-platform connections, bridge entrance members, a staircase beyond a platform end, workshop/rail overlaps, a fence across sidings, intrusive OHE foundations, old doorway proxies, misaligned pit walkways, raised utility crossings, and overlapping rail/formation/bearer geometry.

Independent revised-source service checks found:
- NCJ complete walkway footprints at least 1.5055 m from mapped rail centrelines; hose paths at least 1.4999 m. Five inferred clear pit lengths are approximately 414, 300, 408, 279 and 312 m. Final OHE reporting gives 3.4064 m minimum leg-centre clearance.
- ERS exposed water runs and depth transitions at least 2.2399 m from mapped rail centrelines; other crossings are explicitly recessed below the track formation.
- TVC services and OHE were changed to all-route clearance filtering; corrected actual pointwork proofs show the previous trough/formation obstruction removed.

These numbers describe model geometry or source-placement tests, not approved railway loading or electrical clearances.

## Source and accuracy limits
No authenticated signed historical station-yard plan or complete measured room plan was recovered. Current OSM geometry contains mixed edit dates; edits are not construction dates. TVC uses 2022 architectural references, ERS uses 2017 references, and NCJ combines older frontage references with later map geometry. None is a single-date exact as-built replica.

Room partitions, many fixtures, signals, operational labels, pit-road assignments, detailed switch designs, some maintenance structures and two NCJ missing-map connections are reconstructed. Compound pointwork is explicitly simplified where exact design evidence is unavailable. Nominal gauge and physical detail do not make these models suitable for railway engineering or operations.
