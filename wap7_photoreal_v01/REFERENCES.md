# Reference provenance and interpretation

Reference photographs/manuals are linked for provenance and are **not redistributed** in this package. No source photograph pixels were used as texture maps or backgrounds. Geometry, material maps, lettering assets and the simplified shed emblem were created for this asset.

## Primary numbered-vehicle visual reference

**WAP-7 39002, Royapuram** — WAM4ajj, own photograph, 21 November 2020, CC BY-SA 4.0.

- [Original photographer's Commons file page](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:RPM_WAP-7.jpg)
- [Full-resolution original](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/8/8a/RPM_WAP-7.jpg)
- [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)

Inspected pixels support the chosen white/vermilion scheme; SR/RPM/ROYAPURAM/39002 identity; large dark English side lettering and red side number; dark rounded cab crown; paired guarded windscreens; central twin headlights and vertically paired corner markers; roof-mounted lamp/horns; dense dusty undergear; rubbed buffer faces; front electrical fittings and hanging hoses.

Perspective does not establish precise dimensions. The unseen opposite side and small unresolvable fittings are representative. The shed emblem is simplified original artwork, not a traced or authenticated official crest.

## Class-level construction comparisons

These photographs informed class-level hardware and material interpretation. Their numbers/sheds were not transplanted into the selected identity.

- [GMO WAP 7 30544](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:GMO_WAP_7_30544.jpg) — Anonymous in the world, own photograph, 20 October 2021, CC BY-SA 4.0. Complete flank/cab side/roof: sparse asymmetric intake placement, a large four-panel screen on the other photographed flank, narrow intake, small louvres, cab door/window relation, roof runoff and dusty lower paint
- [WAP7 AP SKE](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:WAP7_AP_SKE.jpg) — Nikhil B, own photograph, 6 May 2016, CC BY-SA 4.0 with an additional attribution-only permission statement. Unit 30317: complete nose, windscreen cages, marker plates, coupler/pilot and substantial bogie/suspension mass
- [WAP-7 outside Mumbai Central](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:WAP-7_Locomotive_outside_Mumbai_Central_Passenger_station.jpg) — Historical Trains, own photograph, 20 May 2023, CC0. Unit 37490: sparse flank equipment, low roof cabinets, ribbed insulators and folded ochre pantographs. The photograph's fence obscures undergear, so it is not used as a bogie dimension source

## Authoritative dimensions and construction context

- [North Western Railway 2025 Working Time Table, technical data p166](https://nwr.indianrailways.gov.in/uploads/files/1742970525725-9%20-%20Working%20Time%20Table.pdf): WAP-7 nominal length over couplers 20.562 m, maximum width 3.152 m, locked-down reference height 4.255 m, 1.676 m gauge, 1.092 m new-wheel tread diameter, 3.700 m bogie wheelbase, 15.700 m total wheelbase. The repository's accepted coupling planes are a separate modelling convention
- [RDSO, Brake Rigging of WAP7 Locomotive](https://rdso.indianrailways.gov.in/works/uploads/File/Brake%20Rigging%20of%20WAP7%20Locomotive(1).pdf): adjacent axle spacing 1,850 mm and bogie overall length 5,930 mm; conventional converted brake rigging with four cylinders per bogie and connecting rods/levers. The model's small brake details are representative, not a certified unit-specific brake-system reconstruction
- [CLW shell specification, CLW-MS-3-152 Alt-13](https://clw.indianrailways.gov.in/uploads/FINAL%20DRAFT%20CLW-MS-3-152%20ALT-13.pdf): RAL9003 white/red-border context; distinct LH/RH side-wall assemblies, rain gutters, wipers, cowcatcher and tightlock transition CBC construction context
- [RDSO WAP-7 upgrade specification, §3.1.28](https://rdso.indianrailways.gov.in/works/uploads/File/17042017_Final_draft_Specification_%20Upgradation_WAP7%281%29.pdf): roof grid-filter radiator intake and upper bodyside filtration context. Used to avoid decorative, uniformly repeated radiator grilles across the entire flank
- [RDSO SMI292, AM92/IR03H pantographs](https://rdso.indianrailways.gov.in/works/uploads/File/SMI%20292%283%29.pdf): original pantograph-v04's maintenance/reference context. The retained authoring mechanism is a simplified level-head rig, not an exact make/subtype reproduction

The 180 km/h speed in this repository is a gameplay choice. It is not evidence that the source should depict WAP-7HS or a particular upgraded physical subtype.
