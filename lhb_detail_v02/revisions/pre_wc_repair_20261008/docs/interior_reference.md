# Interior finishing references and boundaries

## Railway manual

The CAMTECH Maintenance Manual of LHB Coaches first-class layout shows individual reading lights and sockets, compartment/coupe divisions and berth arrangement. Its maintenance inventory explicitly includes compartment mirrors, magazine pockets, coat hooks and curtains. The chair-car sections 1.6, 1.7 and 1.9 distinguish roller blinds from curtains, aluminium/glass luggage racks, reading lights/hooks, ergonomic upholstery, folding tables, footrests and magazine nets. The 450 mm chair cushion height is relative to the selected 1.303 m floor datum.

The SL and GS drawing plates (printed introduction pages 17–18) show three ceiling fans per bay. The legacy GS plan reverses its main/side seating banks across the central entry. These features were checked as pixels, then independently verified in generated geometry.

Official manual: https://secr.indianrailways.gov.in/uploads/files/1622203445123-MMLHB.pdf

## Original photographic observation

Soumendra Kumar Sahoo, *1AC coupe compartment*, own photograph dated 2 December 2023: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:1AC_coupe_compartment.jpg

Actual pixels were inspected. They corroborate rolled burgundy/brown upholstery shoulders, supported upper-berth guard, mesh magazine pockets, above-window mirror and switches/sockets, high luggage shelf, gathered curtains and small stair treads with tubular supports. The image is CC BY-SA 4.0; it is used as a visual reference only and no image pixels are redistributed, projected or embedded in the model. The original geometric model remains an interpretation of a conventional LHB family, not an exact reconstruction of the photographed coupe or its manufacture date.

Earlier direct IRFCA/Flickr images were not available (403/429), so no claim is made to have inspected their pixels. They are not the evidence for the new fittings.

## Modelling scope

Padding, stitch/cord radius, cloth folds, lamp/control dimensions, mirrors, latches, hooks and rack details are original reference-informed shapes. The seated/berth data remain separate from native-game payload and runtime character collision. The source retains explicit capacity and placement checks; it does not certify comfort, load capacity or egress.
