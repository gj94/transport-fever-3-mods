# WAG-9 research references

Research date: 2026-09-30. Links are references, not bundled assets. All delivered geometry, typography meshes and palette materials are newly authored.

## Principal dimensions / primary railway documents

1. Chittaranjan Locomotive Works, **Driver’s Manual for WAG9**, Ident. No. 3EHW411172 Ver-1, chapter 2 page 4, dimension drawing/table. Public mirror: https://st2.indiarailinfo.com/kjfdsuiemjvcya0/0/6/5/5/1427655/0/driversmanualforwag99hclw10964381.pdf
   - 12,000 mm bogie centres; 20,562 mm length over buffers; 1,850 mm adjacent axle spacing; 3,152 mm overall width; 4,255 mm maximum pantograph-locked-down height; 1,676 mm gauge
   - Table and source identification were available in web search extraction; direct PDF retrieval was unavailable in this execution, so this is not a claim to have inspected every drawing page
2. Indian Railways technical training material, **Three Phase Technology**, technical particulars for WAG9, p28. Public mirror: https://st2.indiarailinfo.com/kjfdsuiemjvcya0/0/1/7/7/1406177/0/bookonthreephasetechnologybyir2614763.pdf
   - 1,092 mm new wheels; 19,280 mm length over headstock; 15,700 mm overall axle wheelbase; Co-Co arrangement
3. RDSO **Specification and Standard for IGBT based WAG9**, https://rdso.indianrailways.gov.in/works/uploads/File/Standard_%20IGB_based%20WAG9.pdf
   - Primary railway confirmation of Co-Co arrangement and 1,092 / 1,016 mm new/worn wheel diameters
   - Used for mechanical principal dimensions only, not to imply this legacy-livery model is an exact IGBT variant
4. RDSO pneumatic specification, locomotive length table, https://rdso.indianrailways.gov.in/uploads/files/Draft%20Spec_126%20Rev_1%20of%20JDSE_Pn.pdf
   - Independent railway document confirmation of 20,562 mm WAG9 length
5. RDSO/ABB **WAG-9 Fault Finding Manual**, https://rdso.indianrailways.gov.in/works/uploads/File/Vol.C%20FAULT%20FINDING%20MANUALOF%20WAG-9.pdf
   - Two cab diagnosis terminals; legacy cab diagnostic/instrument vocabulary

## Cab detail reference / limitations

- IRFCA, **Horn lever inside WAG9 31132**, Sandeep Marzara, https://irfca.org/gallery/Locos/Electric/wag9x/Bunty_indiatrip_2007-08%2B081.jpg.html
- IRFCA WAG-9 collection, https://irfca.org/gallery/Locos/Electric/wag9x/

The cab reference image search described legacy pressure gauges, electrical meters, horn/brake levers and ZPT/BLDJ/BLCP/BLHO switches. The gallery image itself was inaccessible in this browser. The delivered cab therefore intentionally uses a documented illustrative legacy arrangement; it is not a traced or surveyed cab reconstruction. Controls and labels are visual details only.
