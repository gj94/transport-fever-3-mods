## Excluded or non-modelled evidence
2023–25 Commons platform views and 2024–26 proposed yard/redevelopment descriptions are not used as proof of the 2010 layout. The third-party-hosted purported Stage-II/2024 Annexure does not authenticate commissioning. The Commons file with lower-case “junction railway station.jpg” credits Google Maps and was not used. Nagercoil Town (NJT) and Kanniyakumari (CAPE) are different stations.

This asset contains no copied satellite imagery, commercial map geometry, downloaded third-party 3D models, or reproduced advertisement artwork. Generic interior/platform/vehicle/vegetation details are original contextual geometry and are identified as such.
